# Customer Intake Script

Use this on every call or message. Goal: get the address and the scope, send a written quote within an hour.

## Phone / text

**Greeting:** "Delta Pressure Washing, this is [name]. How can I help?"

**Get:**
1. Name and best phone number.
2. Property address ("so I can pull it up on the map and get you an exact price").
3. What they want cleaned. Walk through the list: house, driveway, walks, patio, fence, deck, gutters, anything else.
4. Type of siding (vinyl, brick, wood, metal) and how many stories.
5. Any problem areas: "Is there anything in particular bothering you? Green on the north side? Black streaks on the gutters?"
6. Water on site? Spigot working?
7. When they're hoping to have it done.
8. How they heard about you (track this).

**Set expectations:**
- "I'll measure it on satellite and text you a written quote within the hour."
- "Everything's soft wash on the house, so no damage to siding or paint. Driveway gets the surface cleaner."
- "Price includes all chemicals and cleanup, and I'll send you before and after photos."

**Close:** "Any questions before I put the quote together?"

## Upsell lines (use one, not all)

- "While I'm there with everything set up, I can add the driveway for $110 instead of $150."
- "Most folks do the house and driveway together; it's $275 for both."
- "Your neighbors on [street]: if two of you book the same day, I take $25 off each."

## Objection handling

| They say | You say |
|---|---|
| "That's more than I thought." | "I understand. The house alone is $[x] if you want to start there and do the driveway later. I'm also happy to break it into two visits." |
| "My cousin has a pressure washer." | "Sure, lots of people do. The difference is the soft wash chemical and the surface cleaner, which is what gets it even and keeps it clean for a year or more. And I'm insured if anything goes wrong." |
| "Can you do it cheaper for cash?" | "Price is the same either way. I keep it simple." |
| "Let me think about it." | "Of course. The quote's good for 30 days. I'll check back in a few days." (Then do exactly one follow-up.) |

## After the quote

- Day 3, no reply: "Hey [name], just checking if you had any questions on the quote. I have [day] open if you'd like it." 
- No reply after that: leave it. Add to the spring/fall reminder list.
