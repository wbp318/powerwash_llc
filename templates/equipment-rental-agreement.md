## 2. Rent

*Choose one and strike the others.*

- **Option A, per job:** $25 per job for the pressure washer, $10 per job for the trailer, $0.50 per mile for the truck. Renter provides a job count and mileage log monthly.
- **Option B, flat monthly:** $150 per month for the pressure washer, $50 per month for the trailer, $0.50 per mile for the truck.

Rent is paid on the 1st of each month for the prior month, by [Zelle / check].

## 3. Renter's responsibilities

- Fuel, pump oil, and routine maintenance during the rental.
- Repair of any damage caused by Renter's use. Normal wear is not Renter's responsibility.
- Keep the equipment on Renter's general liability insurance as non-owned equipment where the insurer allows.
- Return equipment clean and in the same condition, less normal wear.

## 4. Owner's responsibilities

- Equipment is in safe working order at the start.
- Owner keeps any existing insurance on the truck and trailer; Renter is listed as a permitted driver if required.

## 5. Term and end

- Month to month. Either party can end with 30 days' notice.
- Renter intends to purchase its own pressure washer within 12–18 months and return the Owner's.

## 6. Liability

Renter is responsible for the equipment while in Renter's possession and for any claim arising from Renter's work. Owner is not a party to Renter's customer agreements.

## 7. Taxes

Renter will issue Owner a Form 1099-MISC for rent paid if it exceeds $600 in a calendar year. Owner reports rental income.
