# Chemical Mix Card

Base chemical: **sodium hypochlorite (SH), 12.5%** from a pool supply or chemical distributor. Household bleach is 6–8% and costs twice as much per unit of chlorine; don't use it except in a pinch (double the amount).

Downstream injectors dilute roughly **10:1** (10 parts water to 1 part bucket mix). The "at the wall" column is what actually hits the surface.

| Job | Bucket mix (5 gal) | At the wall (after injector) | Dwell | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| House wash, light mildew | 1 gal SH + 4 gal water + 2 oz surfactant | ~0.25% SH | 5–10 min | Standard vinyl / brick |
| House wash, heavy green/black | 2 gal SH + 3 gal water + 2 oz surfactant | ~0.5% SH | 10 min, reapply | North walls, shaded sides |
| Concrete pre-treat | 2 gal SH + 3 gal water + 2 oz surfactant | ~0.5% | 10 min | Then surface clean |
| Concrete post-treat | 1 gal SH + 4 gal water | ~0.25% | Leave on | Keeps it bright longer |
| Wood fence / deck pre-treat | 1 gal SH + 4 gal water + 2 oz surfactant | ~0.25% | 10 min | Rinse thoroughly, SH can lighten wood |
| Gutter exterior brightening | Gutter cleaner concentrate per label (sodium hydroxide based), applied by pump sprayer | Full strength per label | 2–5 min | Wear gloves + goggles, scrub with brush, rinse |
| Roof (Year 2) | Pump system, not downstream: 3 gal SH + 2 gal water + surfactant | 3–6% direct | Until brown turns gone | No rinse on shingles; rinse plants heavily |
| Fleet / equipment degrease | Degreaser per label, usually 1:10 in pump sprayer | | 5–10 min | High pressure rinse, keep off bare aluminum |
| Pump sprayer spot treat | 1 qt SH + 1 gal water | 2.5% | 5 min | Stubborn spots on siding or concrete |

## Rules

- Mix outdoors. Add SH to water, not water to SH.
- Never mix SH with ammonia, acids, or any other cleaner.
- SH loses strength in heat and sunlight. Buy what you'll use in 2–4 weeks. Store in shade with the lid on.
- Label every bucket and jug.
- Respirator (with chlorine-rated cartridges) and goggles whenever spraying.
- Rinse any overspray off cars, windows, and metal immediately.
- Keep the SDS (safety data sheet) for SH, surfactant, degreaser, and gutter cleaner in the truck.
- If SH gets on skin: rinse 15 minutes with water. In eyes: eyewash 15 minutes, then urgent care.
